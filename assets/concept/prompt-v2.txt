Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: original wide landscape concept art for Hexa Frontier v0.2, a browser hex-grid strategy game with land, naval and air forces plus money and oil infrastructure.
Primary request: depict a near-future fictional industrial coastline as a readable combined-arms military strategy concept, drawing on the general visual language of traditional military strategy maps, with original unit designs and no copied game assets.
Scene/backdrop: hexagonal rocky land formations, a blue ocean bay, sandy coast, an industrial town, a sawtooth-roof tank factory, a military harbor with a gantry crane, a coastal runway, a land oil pump and a distant offshore oil platform. Buildings should have clear recognizable silhouettes without excessive machinery.
Subject: friendly pale olive-gray medium and light tanks and small human rifle infantry squads on land; a slender destroyer and a heavier three-turret warship in the water; a small landing craft with infantry and an amphibious light tank approaching the shore; three distinct aircraft in the upper part of the scene, a compact helicopter with cross-shaped rotor, a swept-wing fighter, and a long-range four-engine bomber with broad straight wings. Distant opposing vehicles and ships have subtle coral-red identification marks.
Style/medium: cohesive stylized 3D / painted military concept, simplified beveled hard-surface models, visible human infantry, readable silhouettes, restrained weathering, low-saturation terrain. More grounded near-future military equipment than space robots. Not photorealistic.
Composition/framing: wide 16:9 landscape, high overhead three-quarter camera that includes land and sea in one frame; units remain readable at medium size, infrastructure creates context rather than filling the entire frame. No user interface, no legend, not a collage, not a sprite sheet.
Lighting/mood: soft clear daylight, slight coastal haze, warm pale sun highlights and small teal friendly identification lights.
Color palette: desaturated olive and stone-gray military armor, deep blue-teal ocean, warm beige shoreline, muted industrial buildings, teal friendly markings, restrained coral opponent markings.
Constraints: original fictional setting and designs, no real flags or historical insignia, no logos, no text, no watermark. The image illustrates artistic atmosphere rather than precise movement, costs, scale or combat rules.
